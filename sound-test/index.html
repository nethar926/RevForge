Immersive phase test

Play once with Immersive Sound on High, once with it Off. Beeps mark each section.

  1. 0:00 front left
  2. 0:05 front right
  3. 0:11 front center
  4. 0:17 rear (out of phase)
  5. 0:23 sweep front to rear
  6. 0:34 sweep rear to front
  7. 0:45 center and rear back and forth

With Immersive on: does section 4 and the sweeps move behind you? With it off: does section 4 sound hollow or "inside your head"?
