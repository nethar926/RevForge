# Carrier Jet switch + lamp art

Wilson Nicklaus' Pixelmator art, cut to transparent WebP (~2x display, captions dropped).
`switches/` toggles (up/neutral/down × lit/unlit), red/black guarded toggles and closed guards,
on nut-centred canvases. `lamps/` master caution/warning (board/window), small round amber/green
(off/powered/lit), Push Square full/1/3 lamps and push-square keys. Mapped in `src/packs/mounts/cjArt.ts`.
Round 2 (Oct 19): MASTER lamps are square (`*-sq`, true aspect); `small-red-*` (GEN) is the amber lamp re-hued to red.
